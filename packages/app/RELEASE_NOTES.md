# DM Command Center release notes

Newest version first. Each "## version" section becomes the text of that GitHub release,
and the app shows it when it offers the update. Add a section and raise "version" in
package.json to publish an update.

## 1.1.0

- **Player pack:** on the Party page, pick the quests, NPCs and places your players know about and save them as a file. Send it to your players (Discord, email, USB); they open it in the new DM Command Center - Player Companion and everything lands in their journal. Sending a newer pack later updates what they have instead of adding duplicates. Descriptions are left out unless you turn them on, so your secrets stay yours.

## 1.0.0

The first official release of DM Command Center, a desktop app for running Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition (2014) games.

**Built on DM Hero.** DM Command Center is based on [DM Hero](https://github.com/Flo0806/dm-hero) by Florian Heuberger, released under the MIT License. His work is the foundation this version is built on. Thank you, Florian.

What this version adds:

- **Rules reference:** @-tag spells, monsters and rules (SRD 5.1) in your notes, with a quick-reference sidebar, your own homebrew, and per-campaign changes
- **Party:** D&D Beyond characters (public sheets) with live HP, AC, passives and conditions, or type everything in by hand; Quick info on each player
- **Ctrl+K search** across everything in the campaign
- **Quests and session continuity**, plus a **Live Session** page
- **Combat:** reference monsters, party HP/AC, death saves, an action log with undo, recharge and limited-use abilities, and typed-in physical dice rolls
- **Templates:** duplicate anything and start new entries from your own templates
- **Player handouts** and item handouts (via Homebrewery)
- **Import your notes** from Word, Markdown or text files, split into the app's sections
- **TaleSpire:** dice links and a DM panel inside TaleSpire
- **Backups** that stay safe while the app is running, and smoother drawing on NVIDIA desktops
- **Updates:** the app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it

The original DM Hero copyright and MIT license are kept in the app (Settings > More > About) and in the LICENSE file.
